**Welcome to the W5133 Multi-State Research Project - Economic Valuation and Management of Natural Resources on Public and Private Lands**


The core mission of the W5133 research group (and all prior iterations) has been to contribute to advances in the theory and empirical application of economic valuation of nonmarket goods and services.  These values are essential inputs to decision-making for public policy that attempt to balance sustainable economic development, rural livelihoods, environmental quality, natural resource use, and conservation goals to support a variety of societal needs. The USDA, through its support of W5133 objectives, is at the forefront in recognizing the need for the improvement of valuation methods, increasing the range of ecosystem service valuation applications, and the development and testing of policy instruments that use valuation information in efforts to efficiently allocate nonmarket ecosystem goods and services.


Members of the W5133 Multi-State Research Project organize an annual meeting where recent economic valuation research is presented by academic faculty, graduate students, and government and industry researchers. Recent annual meeting agendas and W5133 objectives can be found in [Past Meetings]({{ '/past-meetings/' | relative_url }}). If you are interested in attending the upcoming annual meeting, join the mailing list by completing [this form](https://forms.gle/nvUsDyn1hoSc3K567). W5133 Research Project membership is not required to present and/or attend the annual meeting.
