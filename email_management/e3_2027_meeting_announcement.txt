<p>Hello Members and Friends of W5133!</p>

<p>The <strong>2027 W5133 meetings will take place in Fort Collins, Colorado, on March 25&ndash;26.</strong> We will begin with a social gathering on Wednesday evening, March 24, followed by full days of sessions on Thursday and Friday, March 25 and 26.
