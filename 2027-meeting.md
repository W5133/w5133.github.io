## Fort Collins, Colorado · March 24–26, 2027

Join us to discuss the economic valuation and management of
natural resources. Formal W5133 membership is not required to present or attend.

- **Thursday and Friday, March 25–26:** Full days of research sessions.
- **Venue:** [Center for Creativity](https://www.fortcollins.gov/Venues/C4C), 200 Mathews Street, Fort Collins, CO 80524.

[Submit an abstract](https://forms.gle/Rf2KKbMf63APgNi2A) ·
[Book a room at the Elizabeth Hotel](https://app.marriott.com/resview2?id=1789145728352&key=GRP&app=resvlink)

## Abstract Submission

**Abstracts are due December 15, 2026.**

Use the [2027 workshop abstract submission form](https://forms.gle/Rf2KKbMf63APgNi2A)
to provide your paper title, presenter information, coauthors, brief abstract, and relevant
W5133 research tasks. The form accepts abstracts up to 4,000 characters (approximately 500 words).
